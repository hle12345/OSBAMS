## 2. Electrical review

### R1 — Diode polarity (CRITICAL)

Schematic: `OSBAMS:D_H` and `OSBAMS:D_TVS` draw the triangle base at pin 1 → **pin 1 = anode, pin 2 = cathode**. PCB footprints `D_DO-201AD_P15.24mm_Horizontal` and `D_SMB` are KiCad library parts whose **pad 1 = cathode** (square pad, 'K' on silk, band at pad 1; D1's silk bar at pad 1). Resulting connections:

| Diode | Role | Schematic intent | As built on the PCB |
|---|---|---|---|
| D3 SB560 | series reverse-polarity | anode 12V_IN, cathode +12V | cathode (pad 1) on 12V_IN, anode on +12V → blocks the supply |
| D2 1N5408 | coil flyback | anode COIL_SW, cathode COIL_V | cathode (pad 1) on COIL_SW, anode on COIL_V → conducts +12V→Q1→GND when Q1 turns on, coil bypassed |
| D1 SMBJ15A | TVS on +12V | cathode +12V, anode GND | cathode (pad 1) on GND, anode on +12V → forward-conducts the rail to GND |

Fix in KiCad (symbol pin numbering or footprint/net swap), update the PCB from the schematic, re-run DRC/ERC. **Not changed here.** ERC does not catch this because the symbol pins are 'passive'.

### R2 — Scope gap versus the requested controller functions (CRITICAL for the intended use)

| Requested review item | Finding |
|---|---|
| STM32 decoupling | N/A — the STM32L476RG is on the external NUCLEO-L476RG. Only a 100 nF (C3) sits on +3V3 near J4/J6, C2 at the TC74. |
| INA228 layout / Kelvin sensing | N/A on this PCB — INA228 is an external breakout; its shunt/VBUS wiring (Kelvin routing) is off-board. J4 carries 3V3/GND/SCL/SDA only. |
| Analog/digital grounding | one B.Cu solid GND pour; 12 V coil return current (Q1 source → pour → J1) shares it with logic ground; no split. Acceptable for this simple board; no analog front end exists. |
| ADC routing | **none** — no independent ADC voltage divider on the PCB (firmware/docs expect one). |
| Power rails | +12V (after D3), +3V3 (from Nucleo via J5.1). **No 5 V rail, no regulator.** |
| Protection circuits | D3 reverse-polarity, D1 TVS (15 V standoff), D2 flyback, C1 bulk — all subject to R1. No input fuse on the PCB (external F2 control fuse assumed); no ESD protection on UART/I2C headers. |
| Relay driver | Q1 IRLZ44N low-side, R1 220 Ω gate, R2 10 kΩ pull-down, D2 flyback. Gate drive from a 3.3 V GPIO (J5.5); IRLZ44N is logic-level but Rds(on) is specified at 4–5 V — fine for a small coil, not verified here. Default state: gate pulled low = coil off (good). |
| E-stop monitoring | **loop only** (J3 in series with the coil supply — hardware interruption works). **No E-stop status line to the MCU**; firmware expects PA0. |
| Relay feedback / ARM input / USB / programming header / test points | **absent** |

### R3 — Other electrical observations

- **Track widths:** every track is 0.3 mm including +12V/COIL_V/COIL_SW; the 1.2 mm 'Powerpath' net class was not applied (patterns lack the leading '/'). Coil current is not specified in the repo.
- **C1** 25 V electrolytic vs the TVS clamp (~24 V at SMBJ15A peak) — thin margin; consider 35 V.
- **I2C:** TC74A5 at 0x4D, INA228 at 0x40 — no address conflict; pull-ups are not on this PCB (they are on the Nucleo/breakout side — confirm).
- **U1 annular ring** 0.087 mm on the narrow axis — below the typical 0.15 mm fab minimum.
- **J5** described as keyed; footprint is a plain header.

| U1 | CRITICAL | Diode polarity mismatch: D1 (TVS), D2 (flyback), D3 (reverse-polarity) | Schematic symbols (OSBAMS:D_H, OSBAMS:D_TVS) have pin 1 = anode, pin 2 = cathode.
