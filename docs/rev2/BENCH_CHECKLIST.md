# Bench checklist — exact physical tests, in order

Generated by `tools/gen_rev2_docs.py` from `desktop/equipment/validation.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Order: records → bench bring-up with no battery → pack connected, relay open, no load → first supervised low-current load → full characterization of the 36–42 V packs. Equipment: the **6060B** (the actual battery load, manual until GPIB is confirmed), the **EDU34450A** (reference measurement) and the **EDUX1052G** (validation-only observation), plus the OSBAMS stack itself. Status of every step: **NOT_RUN**. Nothing here is BENCH_TESTED or HARDWARE_VALIDATED. Write each acceptance tolerance into the record *before* the run. Track results with `equipment.validation.ValidationLog` (PASS needs recorded data and the prerequisite steps PASS).


## A. Records

### A1 — Record identity of the reference instruments
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN
- **Equipment:** 6060B, EDU34450A, EDUX1052G
- **Procedure:** Read model, serial, asset ID and calibration-due date off each instrument. Enter them in equipment/inventory.py.
- **Pass criterion:** No asset ID or calibration status left UNKNOWN for the 6060B or the EDU34450A.

### A2 — Inspect the OSBAMS power path and wiring
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A (continuity / resistance)
- **Procedure:** Read the exact Durakool DG57CM part number, shunt part number, fuse and fuse-holder part numbers and the 12->5 V DC/DC converter(s) (the four open items in HARDWARE_FREEZE_CANDIDATE.md). Check polarity labelling, wire gauge, XT60 connector and that the shunt is in series. Continuity and polarity with the EDU34450A, power OFF, nothing connected.
- **Pass criterion:** Every power-path rating written into capability.PowerPathLimits or marked unspecified.


## B. Bench bring-up (no battery)

### B1 — Controller and sensor bring-up
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** STM32L476RG on USB power, Raspberry Pi 5 / PC running OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Power the STM32 board from USB. Start the OSBAMS serial reader. Confirm INA228 and TC74 report and that Protocol v1 frames arrive with valid CRC and incrementing sequence numbers.
- **Pass criterion:** Both sensors healthy in the BOOT/SENSOR frames; frames parse with valid CRC.

### B2 — Fault handling that needs no source
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B1
- **Equipment:** OSBAMS only (no battery, no load)
- **Procedure:** Unplug a sensor; unplug the host link; press the E-stop; exercise relay feedback; trigger over-temperature by lowering the temperature limit and warming the TC74; trigger over-voltage by setting a profile maximum below a measured value.
- **Pass criterion:** Firmware reaches FAULT in each case and the load-enable output goes OFF.

### B3 — Relay coil-side timing
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B1
- **Equipment:** EDUX1052G (rated probes, good grounding)
- **Procedure:** Capture load-enable (PC8), coil drive and aux feedback (PC9). Measure close and open time and E-stop-to-coil-open with the MCU held in reset; >= 10 repetitions. No battery voltage on the relay contacts.
- **Pass criterion:** Times recorded; the coil de-energises on E-stop with the firmware dead.


## C. Pack connected, relay open, no load

### C1 — First pack connection and OCV verification (Ninebot NEB1002-H, 5.2 Ah)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A2, B2, B3
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A (polarity before connecting, then OCV), OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Gate: A2, B2, B3 PASS. Inspect the pack, check polarity with the EDU34450A, connect with the relay OPEN and no load. Compare OSBAMS INA228 voltage with the EDU34450A and record with equipment.reference.make_record('voltage', ...).
- **Pass criterion:** Record saved; error shown; tolerance written beforehand. Registry row complete (chemistry, cutoff).

### C2 — Independent ADC cross-check
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C1
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A
- **Procedure:** Compare the independent ADC voltage with the INA228 and the EDU34450A at the same moment. Confirm the disagreement fault fires when the threshold is deliberately set below the measured difference (test configuration).
- **Pass criterion:** Fault raised at the injected threshold; no false fault at the normal threshold.

### C3 — Temperature sensor check
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C1
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A (temperature function), TC74
- **Procedure:** Compare ambient with the EDU34450A. Thermal protection is NOT validated until the TC74 works and is attached at a suitable point on the pack.
- **Pass criterion:** Record saved; 1 C TC74 resolution acknowledged.

### C4 — Software envelope vs the 6060B panel
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C1
- **Equipment:** Dashboard permitted-current panel, 6060B front panel
- **Procedure:** With the pack voltage shown, read FINAL PERMITTED and the limiting factor. Confirm it equals min(profile, 10 A, 60 A, 300 W / conservative V). Load input stays OFF. Do NOT deliberately exceed 300 W on the instrument.
- **Pass criterion:** Panel value matches the formula at the measured pack voltage.


## D. First supervised load (low current)

### D1 — Low-current measurement comparison
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C2, C3, C4
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual CC, <= 0.5 A and <= FINAL PERMITTED), EDU34450A in series (check its current range/fuse first), OSBAMS, EDUX1052G
- **Procedure:** Gate: C2, C3, C4 PASS. Close the relay, set CC on the 6060B panel and enable the load. Record current from OSBAMS, the 6060B panel and the EDU34450A at 0.1, 0.25 and 0.5 A, and voltage spot checks. Capture load-enable-to-current onset.
- **Pass criterion:** Records saved with 6060B readback and reference; discrepancies listed; decide if the existing shunt is adequate at 1-3 A.

### D2 — E-stop and shutdown timing under load
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D1
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual), EDUX1052G
- **Procedure:** At <= 0.5 A press the E-stop and trigger a firmware fault; measure fault-to-current-zero and E-stop-to-open; observe voltage transients.
- **Pass criterion:** Times recorded; load current reaches ~0; no unexpected transient.


## E. Full characterization (36-42 V packs)

### E1 — Orchestrated CC capacity test (NEB1002-H)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D2
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual CC <= the dashboard's FINAL PERMITTED, profile 1 A), OSBAMS, EDU34450A spot checks
- **Procedure:** Run Capacity: OCV -> safety check -> READY -> confirm -> discharge to the profile cutoff -> load OFF verified -> recovery. Spot-check voltage with the EDU34450A at start, mid and end (record each).
- **Pass criterion:** Automatic cutoff; load-off confirmed; Ah/Wh stored; spot-checks recorded; temperature below limit.

### E2 — DCIR current-step test (NEB1002-H)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E1
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual), OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Run DCIR as a separate test on a rested pack; repeat 3 times with rest between.
- **Pass criterion:** Three results recorded; repeatability reported (no tolerance claimed).

### E3 — Thermal observation
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E1
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A temperature function, TC74
- **Procedure:** During E1/E2 compare pack-surface temperature (reference) with the TC74; record peak.
- **Pass criterion:** Peak temperature recorded; stop-on-limit behaviour verified or marked untested.

### E4 — Repeat on Shenzhen Elite (12.8 Ah) then NEE1006-M (15.3 Ah)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E2
- **Equipment:** as C1-E3 for each pack
- **Procedure:** Same sequence including the relay-open OCV verification. Packs with BMS anomalies in the intake notes (blinking blue, cut wire) are inspected and judged safe BEFORE any connection.
- **Pass criterion:** Per-pack records saved; no pack connected without an inspection note.

### E5 — Passport and report reproducibility
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E1
- **Equipment:** OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Generate the report and battery passport for each pack, regenerate them, and compare.
- **Pass criterion:** Reports identical except timestamps; version block present.


## F. Remote control (blocked)

### F1 — Identify the SFSU GPIB path
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** lab inventory
- **Procedure:** USB<->GPIB adapter, LAN<->GPIB gateway or GPIB-equipped PC? Record models and the 6060B GPIB address (docs/rev2/GPIB_INTERFACE_CONFIRMATION.md).
- **Pass criterion:** A concrete interface identified and recorded.

### F2 — Verify commands against the official manuals
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** F1
- **Equipment:** Electronic Load Family Programming Reference Manual; 6060B/6063B Operating Manual
- **Procedure:** For each row of 6060B_DRIVER_EVIDENCE.md read the exact command, fill document + section/page, set VERIFIED.
- **Pass criterion:** Operations are enabled only when every command they need is VERIFIED.

### F3 — First remote contact at low current
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** F2, D2
- **Equipment:** 6060B + GPIB path, EDU34450A, pack with relay supervised
- **Procedure:** Construct Keysight6060B(interface_confirmed=True); identify, set_cc (<= 0.5 A), input_on/off, measure; compare with the EDU34450A.
- **Pass criterion:** Readbacks agree with the reference; envelope guard refuses over-limit requests without writing.

### F4 — Transient observation
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** F3
- **Equipment:** 6060B remote, EDUX1052G
- **Procedure:** Only if the transient commands are VERIFIED: configure a low-amplitude transient and observe the current step on the scope.
- **Pass criterion:** Observed levels/frequency match the configuration.
