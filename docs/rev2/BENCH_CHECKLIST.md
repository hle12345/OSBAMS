# Bench checklist — exact physical tests, in order

Generated by `tools/gen_rev2_docs.py` from `desktop/equipment/validation.py`. Do not edit by hand.

Roles: **EDU36311A** low-energy commissioning source · **EDU34450A** reference DMM · **EDUX1052G** transient/shutdown observation · **AD2** low-voltage digital/protocol debugging · **6060B** the actual battery load (manual until GPIB is confirmed).

Status of every step: **NOT_RUN**. Nothing here is BENCH_TESTED or HARDWARE_VALIDATED. Write each acceptance tolerance into the record *before* the run. Track results with `equipment.validation.ValidationLog` (PASS needs recorded data and the prerequisite steps PASS).


## A. Records

### A1 — Record identity of every instrument
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN
- **Equipment:** all instruments
- **Procedure:** Read model, serial, asset ID and calibration-due date off each instrument (6060B, EDU34450A, EDU36311A, EDUX1052G, EDU33212A, AD2, handheld DMM). Enter in equipment/inventory.py.
- **Pass criterion:** No asset ID or calibration status left UNKNOWN for an instrument used as a reference.

### A2 — Inspect the OSBAMS power path and wiring
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN
- **Equipment:** handheld DMM
- **Procedure:** Read the exact Durakool DG57CM part number, shunt part number, fuse and fuse-holder part numbers, and the 12->5 V DC/DC converter(s) (the four open items in HARDWARE_FREEZE_CANDIDATE.md); check polarity labelling, wire gauge, connector type and that the shunt is in series. Continuity and polarity with the handheld DMM, power OFF, no source connected.
- **Pass criterion:** Every power-path rating written into capability.PowerPathLimits or marked unspecified.


## B. Low-energy commissioning

### B1 — Controller, I2C and UART bring-up
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN
- **Equipment:** AD2 (logic/UART/I2C), USB power only
- **Procedure:** Power the STM32 board from USB. Capture I2C: INA228 and TC74 acknowledge at their addresses. Capture UART: Protocol v1 frames decode and CRC-validate (also feed a captured line to services.protocol.parse_frame).
- **Pass criterion:** Both sensors ACK; frames parse with valid CRC; sequence numbers increment.

### B2 — INA228 voltage accuracy
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A (CH2, and CH2+CH3 in series per the manufacturer's series-operation instructions), EDU34450A
- **Procedure:** Step 3, 5, 10, 20, 30 V (and higher via series only after reading the EDU36311A manual). At each step record EDU34450A and OSBAMS voltage with equipment.reference.make_record('voltage', ...).
- **Pass criterion:** Records saved; error shown per point; tolerance written beforehand by the operator.

### B3 — INA228 current accuracy at low current
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B2
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A (CH1 into a resistor), EDU34450A in series (check its current range/fuse first)
- **Procedure:** Drive 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 A through the shunt. Record reference vs OSBAMS current.
- **Pass criterion:** Records saved; error vs current plotted; decide if the existing shunt is adequate at 1-3 A.

### B4 — Temperature sensor check
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A (temperature function), TC74
- **Procedure:** Compare ambient and a warmed point (hand/warm water bath, not a battery).
- **Pass criterion:** Records saved; 1 C TC74 resolution acknowledged in the result.

### B5 — ADC divider cross-check and disagreement fault
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B2
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A, EDU34450A
- **Procedure:** Compare INA228 vs ADC voltage over 5-30 V; inject a deliberate divider error and confirm the disagreement fault fires.
- **Pass criterion:** Fault raised at the injected error; no false fault over the normal range.

### B6 — Threshold and fault-injection tests
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B1
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A, EDU33212A (sensor-signal injection), AD2
- **Procedure:** Drive over-voltage, under-voltage, over-temperature (simulated sensor value), sensor loss, host disconnect.
- **Pass criterion:** Firmware reaches FAULT in each case and the load-enable output goes OFF.

### B7 — Contactor and E-stop timing
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B1
- **Equipment:** EDUX1052G, EDU36311A (low-voltage supply through the contactor into a resistor)
- **Procedure:** Capture coil drive, aux feedback (PC9) and downstream voltage. Measure close time, open time and E-stop-to-open with the MCU held in reset. Repeat >= 10 times. Probe only with rated probes and good grounding.
- **Pass criterion:** Times recorded; contactor opens on E-stop with the firmware dead.


## C. 6060B without a battery

### C1 — 6060B power-up and front-panel record
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual), handheld DMM
- **Procedure:** Run the 6060B power-up self-test. Record the GPIB address setting, input range and protection settings. Use Manual6060B in OSBAMS.
- **Pass criterion:** Self-test passes; settings recorded.

### C2 — Three-way comparison OSBAMS vs 6060B vs EDU34450A
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B2, B3, C1
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A as the source, 6060B (manual, CC), EDU34450A, OSBAMS INA228
- **Procedure:** At 3 voltage points <= 30 V and currents <= 1 A (supply limit), set CC on the 6060B panel. Record voltage and current from all three instruments.
- **Pass criterion:** Records saved with 6060B readback and reference; discrepancies listed.

### C3 — Software envelope vs panel setting
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C1
- **Equipment:** Dashboard permitted-current panel, 6060B panel
- **Procedure:** For 3 voltages read the FINAL PERMITTED value from the dashboard and confirm the operator-entered CC setting never exceeds it. Do NOT deliberately exceed 300 W on the instrument.
- **Pass criterion:** Panel value = min(profile, 10 A, 60 A, 300 W / V) at each voltage.

### C4 — Orchestrator dry run on a supply 'pack'
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C2, C3
- **Equipment:** EDU36311A CH2 as a stand-in source (NOT a battery), 6060B (manual), OSBAMS, dashboard
- **Procedure:** Create a low-voltage registry row (e.g. 12 V, cutoff 9 V). Run the Capacity test: OCV -> READY -> operator enables load -> lower the supply voltage by hand to cross the cutoff -> verify automatic load-off request and recovery. Run DCIR (steps within the 1 A supply limit).
- **Pass criterion:** All phases reached in order; load-off confirmed from measurement; results saved. A supply does not model a battery, so no capacity/DCIR number from this step is meaningful.

### C5 — E-stop / fault during a loaded run
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** B7, C4
- **Equipment:** EDUX1052G, EDU36311A source, 6060B (manual)
- **Procedure:** During C4 press the E-stop; capture current decay and observe the orchestrator report FAULT with verified load-off.
- **Pass criterion:** Current falls to ~0; orchestrator ends FAULT/off-confirmed; times recorded.


## D. Battery tests

### D1 — Intake and OCV of the first pack (Ninebot NEB1002-H, 5.2 Ah)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** C5
- **Equipment:** handheld DMM (polarity), EDU34450A (OCV), OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Inspect, check polarity, measure OCV with the reference DMM, register with a cutoff voltage. Compare OSBAMS OCV.
- **Pass criterion:** Registry row complete with cutoff; OCV agreement recorded.

### D2 — Orchestrated CC capacity test (NEB1002-H)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D1
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual CC <= the dashboard's FINAL PERMITTED, profile 1 A), OSBAMS, EDU34450A spot checks
- **Procedure:** Run Capacity: OCV -> READY -> confirm -> discharge to the profile cutoff -> load OFF verified -> recovery. Spot-check voltage with the EDU34450A at start, mid and end.
- **Pass criterion:** Automatic cutoff; load-off confirmed; Ah/Wh stored; V/I spot-checks recorded; temperature below limit.

### D3 — DCIR current-step test (NEB1002-H)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D2
- **Equipment:** 6060B (manual), OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Run DCIR as a separate test on a rested pack; repeat 3 times with rest between.
- **Pass criterion:** Three results recorded; repeatability reported (no tolerance claimed).

### D4 — Thermal observation
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D2
- **Equipment:** EDU34450A temperature function, TC74
- **Procedure:** During D2/D3 compare pack-surface temperature (reference) with TC74; record peak.
- **Pass criterion:** Peak temperature recorded; stop-on-limit behaviour verified or marked untested. Thermal protection is NOT validated until the TC74 works and is attached at a suitable point.

### D5 — Repeat on Shenzhen Elite (12.8 Ah) then NEE1006-M (15.3 Ah)
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D3
- **Equipment:** as D1-D4
- **Procedure:** Same sequence. Packs with BMS anomalies in the intake notes (blinking blue, cut wire) are inspected and judged safe BEFORE any connection.
- **Pass criterion:** Per-pack records saved; no pack connected without an inspection note.

### D6 — Passport and report reproducibility
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** D2
- **Equipment:** OSBAMS
- **Procedure:** Generate the report for each pack, regenerate it, and compare.
- **Pass criterion:** Reports identical except timestamps; version block present.


## E. Remote control (blocked)

### E1 — Identify the SFSU GPIB path
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** A1
- **Equipment:** lab inventory
- **Procedure:** USB<->GPIB adapter, LAN<->GPIB gateway or GPIB-equipped PC? Record models and the 6060B GPIB address (docs/rev2/GPIB_INTERFACE_CONFIRMATION.md).
- **Pass criterion:** A concrete interface identified and recorded.

### E2 — Verify commands against the official manuals
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E1
- **Equipment:** Electronic Load Family Programming Reference Manual; 6060B/6063B Operating Manual
- **Procedure:** For each row of 6060B_COMMAND_EVIDENCE.md read the exact command, fill document + section/page, set VERIFIED.
- **Pass criterion:** Operations are enabled only when every command they need is VERIFIED.

### E3 — First remote contact on a supply, no battery
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E2
- **Equipment:** 6060B + GPIB path, EDU36311A, EDU34450A
- **Procedure:** Construct Keysight6060B(interface_confirmed=True); identify, set_cc, input_on/off, measure on a supply source; compare with EDU34450A.
- **Pass criterion:** Readbacks agree with the reference; envelope guard refuses over-limit requests without writing.

### E4 — Transient observation
- [ ] **Status:** NOT_RUN · **Requires:** E3
- **Equipment:** 6060B remote, EDUX1052G (current probe/shunt), AD2 optional
- **Procedure:** Configure a low-amplitude transient on a supply source and observe the current step on the scope.
- **Pass criterion:** Observed levels/frequency match the configuration.
