**Status: DESIGN PHASE. No Rev.2 schematic, layout or manufacturing file exists, and none will be created until the critical datasheet inputs are verified** (§14). It supersedes both and `docs/rev2/pcb/REV2_SCHEMATIC_PCB_PLAN.md`.

Tags: **[P]** purchased (invoice / purchase list) · **[R1]** worked in Rev.1 or is a firmware contract · **[C]** candidate, datasheet not yet read · **[N]** new Rev.2 circuit · **[L]** checked in the installed KiCad 7 libraries (not a datasheet).

Envelope: Li-ion 30–42 V, 44 V ceiling, ≤ 10 A operating ceiling, 15 A fuse, RSA-20-50 shunt (2.5 mΩ, 20 A/50 mV), XT60 external, Agilent 6060B, 300 W. **Battery discharge current never enters the board.**

```
Battery → XT60 → 15 A fuse → manual disconnect → K1 contacts → RSA-20-50 → 6060B → Battery−     (external, high current)
12 V → [F1 · Schottky · TVS] → E-stop NC → ARM → K1 coil → Q1 (SMD, low side) → GND                (on this board, ≈ 0.13 A)
```
E-stop and ARM are in the **12 V coil chain only**, never in the battery-current path.

## 2. MCU: direct STM32L476RGT6 (A) — decided

One board, no carrier header (the J5 bug class disappears), routine LQFP-64 turnkey assembly, no ST-LINK/E5V/USB back-power states around the safety MCU, same firmware silicon (HSI16 → PLL 80 MHz, no crystal [R1]). Cost: new first-article risk (power, reset, boot, SWD) — mitigated by a small conventional MCU circuit, SWD header, probing test points, and keeping the Rev.1 Nucleo stack as the working fallback until bring-up passes. Conditions: 2–3 first-article boards; a bring-up checklist (rails, reset, SWD attach, 80 MHz, GPIO, I²C scan, UART echo) written before layout; the firmware change list in §13 reviewed first. B (Nucleo module) is the fallback only if bring-up of A fails.

MCU circuit: all VDD 100 nF (VJ0805Y104JXXAT [P]) + 4.7 µF bulk; VDDA/VREF+ from 3V3A through ferrite + 1 µF + 100 nF; VBAT to VDD; NRST 100 nF + reset button + TP; BOOT0 (PH3) 10 kΩ pull-down + 2-pin jumper; SWD (PA13/PA14, SWO PB3) on J9; **no crystal, no LSE** (DNP HSE footprint optional); PA11/PA12 unused (no native USB).

## 5. Power tree

```
XDR-75-12 (12.0 V) → J1 → F1 1 A fast → reverse-protection Schottky (SMD) → SMBJ15A TVS → +12V
   +12V → E-STOP → ARM → K1 coil → Q1                                 ≈ 0.13 A
   +12V → wide-input buck → 3V3 (digital) → ferrite + C → 3V3A (VDDA, INA228)
```
No 5 V rail exists on the board; the Pi/display use a separate external 5 V supply, and the USB-UART bridge's host side is powered from USB VBUS (isolated). 3V3 load ≈ 40 mA typical, design 150 mA; 12 V rail ≈ 0.19 A total; F1 1 A. Buck choice: wide-input 40 V class (LMR14006 family) preferred because the SMBJ15A clamp (≈ 24.4 V) leaves almost no margin on a 28 V part (TPS54202); both are unverified [C] and the FB divider/inductor/Isat follow the datasheet. Series Schottky drop ≈ 0.4 V → coil at ≈ 11.6 V (relay needs to pull in below that; verify). XDR output is adjustable up to ~15 V: it will be set and verified at 12.0 V (coil 2.5 W at 15 V). Capacitors on +12V rated ≥ 35 V. Details: calculations §7.

## 6. Relay driver and coil chain

`+12V → J2 (E-stop NC) → ESTOP_OUT → J3 (ARM) → COIL_V → J4 (K1 coil) → COIL_SW → Q1 drain`, source GND.
- **K1 = Durakool DG57CM-5021-76-1012-R** [P], 12 V coil ≈ 90 Ω (listing; to be read from the datasheet: coil resistance, pick-up/drop-out, release time, inductance, DC make/break at ≤ 44 V/10 A and 15 A fault). Kept unless the datasheet shows it inadequate.
- **Q1: SMD logic-level N-FET, VDSS ≥ 60 V, RDS(on) specified at VGS 2.5 V and 3.3 V, VGS(th) max ≤ 2.0 V** — part not yet selected (criteria and the dissipation check in calculations §3). **R_G 220 Ω**, **R_PD 10 kΩ gate → GND**: relay OFF whenever the MCU is in reset/unpowered/Hi-Z.
- **Flyback:** 1N5408G [P] across the coil, cathode on COIL_V; first article = plain diode. A 0 Ω link in series with the diode (and a DNP TVS footprint) allows a faster-release clamp later — only after the relay release-time data and the MOSFET VDSS are verified; **no 27 V commitment**.
- Amber LED: COIL_V → 2.2 kΩ → LED → COIL_SW (lights only while Q1 conducts). TPs: GATE, COIL_SW, COIL_V.
- Firmware cannot energise the coil unless J2 and J3 are closed.

## 7. Measurement

**INA228** (VSSOP-10 [C]): IN+ = relay load-side (RELAY_OUT), IN− = shunt load-side Kelvin; 2 × 10 Ω + 100 nF differential filter; 1.5SMBJ48A [P] on IN+ and on PACK_INA (clamp ≈ 77 V vs 85 V abs max: thin margin, flagged); **VBUS from PACK_INA (upstream of K1)** so OCV is readable with the relay open; separate **PACK_ADC** lead for the independent channel; **GND_SENSE is the only logic-GND ↔ pack-negative bond**. ADCRANGE = 0 (±163.84 mV): range 1 would saturate at 16.4 A and hide the 18.5 A trip. IMAX 20 A → CURRENT_LSB 38.147 µA, **SHUNT_CAL 1250** (equation unverified; the current firmware header still uses 30 A/1875 — a firmware change). Shunt: 25 mV/0.25 W at 10 A, 46.25 mV at the firmware trip. Kelvin pair routed tightly on one layer over solid GND, no vias, away from buck/coil/USB.

**Independent ADC channel:** PACK_ADC → 75 k → 75 k → ADC_TAP → 10 k → GND; 1 k; 100 nF; 16:1; 44 V → 2.750 V; 12.9 mV/LSB. Error kept in three groups: **divider ±0.43 % (resistors ±0.19 %, tempco ±0.20 %, leakage)**, ADC ±0.1 %, reference ±2 % uncorrected → ±0.3 % with VREFINT correction → total ±2.5 % uncorrected, ±0.8 % corrected. Only ≈ 0.4 % is the divider — the rest is the 3V3 reference. Plausibility cross-check against INA228 (1.5 V window), not metrology.
**Protection (replaces the BAT54S pair):** 1 kΩ series, 100 nF, a clamp **to GND only**; no connection from the tap to the 3V3 rail. Back-feed analysis (PACK+ present, board off): the external circuit offers no path; if the datasheet shows PA1 has an upper protection diode, a 3.3 kΩ bleeder (DNP footprint) holds the rail at ≈ 0.95 V, below the POR threshold, with ≤ 0.29 mA back-feed. Maximum PA1 voltage: 2.75 V at 44 V, 3.0 V at 48 V, 4.84 V at the 77.4 V TVS clamp (needs the pin's tolerance from the datasheet), ≥ −0.3 V negative with the GND clamp. Clamp leakage error to be taken from the clamp diode's datasheet at 2.75 V (calculations §1).

## 8. E-stop / ARM / relay-feedback

Sensing is read-only; each reads the **real circuit node** so a broken wire shows as a fault.
| Signal | Taps | Circuit | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| ESTOP_SENSE → PA0 | `ESTOP_OUT` (post-E-stop) | VO610A-1 [P] LED fed from the node through a series resistor + 1N4148 antiparallel; collector pull-up to 3V3; 10 nF | LOW = loop closed (healthy); open/broken → HIGH (fail-safe, firmware contract) |
| ARM_SENSE → PC10 | `COIL_V` (post-ARM) | 270 k/100 k divider, 1 k, 100 nF, **no clamp** (PC10 must be a 5 V-tolerant pin; else ratio ≤ 0.22) | HIGH = ARM and E-stop closed; E-stop tripped → ARM unknown |
| RELAY_FB → PC9 | `RELAY_OUT` (downstream of K1) vs `GND_SENSE` | VO610A-1 LED chain of ≥ 2 series resistors + 1N4148; pull-up to 3V3; 10 nF | LOW = pack voltage present after K1 (relay closed); also reveals a welded relay when compared with PACK_ADC |

**Resistor values are not frozen.** The method (calculations §4): CTR_eff = CTR_min at the actual IF × temperature derate × aging derate (all read from the official VO610A-1 data); require IF·CTR_eff ≥ 2× pull-up demand at the minimum voltage (≥ 24–28 V for RELAY_FB, 10.5 V for E-stop). With placeholder derates the Chat networks fail and the Code networks (2×6.8 k + 33 k; 5.1 k + 33 k) pass — an illustration of why the real data is needed, not a selection. Creepage ≥ 2 mm between pack-level (`PK_`) nets and logic.
E-stop contacts: the baseline needs **one** NC circuit through J2. If the installed Eaton block offers a second suitable NC contact it can be wired in series externally for dual-channel opening; no board feature depends on it.
Fault philosophy: any mismatch (e.g. LOAD_EN high but RELAY_FB high for > debounce) is a diagnostic fault; sensing never grants permission.

## 9. Temperature interface

TC74A5-3.3VAT [P] (0x4D) on a separate probe PCB: TO-220-5 on the pack surface + **VJ0805Y104JXXAT 100 nF at VDD (≤ 3 mm)**, keyed JST PH 4-pin (3V3, SDA, SCL, GND), shielded twisted pair **≤ 1.5 m** (≤ 1 m expected). On the controller: **I²C2 (PB10/PB11) separate from INA228**, 100 Ω series, ESD (2-channel), 100 nF, 4.7 kΩ pull-ups (2.2 kΩ option; DNP-able), SDA2/SCL2 TPs, ≤ 100 kHz. Bus budget (calculations §6): 4.7 kΩ gives 0.4–0.8 µs rise to ≈ 215 pF. Firmware: I²C timing for ≤ 100 kHz (the Rev.1 timing is ≈ 125–139 kHz, out of spec), init wait ≥ 300 ms, keep the raw init status. If V1 shows the TC74 is not viable or the cable must exceed ≈ 1.5 m: stop and propose another interface (DS18B20 / NTC are contingency only, not in the baseline schematic).

## 10. Host, ground and isolation

USART2 (PA2/PA3) → ISO7721 → CP2102N → USB-C → Pi 5 (host side powered from VBUS). The Pi's ground never touches the board; SWD probe ground only during debug; cable shields at one end. Pi 5 + touchscreen use their own external 5 V supply (`docs/rev2/pcb/PI_POWER_ARCHITECTURE.md`). Mounting holes isolated (plastic standoffs); chassis earth is not connected on this PCB. Single bond: GND_SENSE (J6 pin 4). The XDR output is isolated and returns through J1.
